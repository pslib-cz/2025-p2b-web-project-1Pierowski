We raise new talents. Enroll your child in our chess club.

Chess club for kids

Chess is an ideal tool for developing logical thinking, concentration, and patience. Our classes are led by experienced licensed coaches who have many years of experience working with children.

Training Schedule

  • Preparation (beginners): Tuesday 15:30 - 16:30
  • Advanced pupils: Thursday 16:00 - 17:30
  • Adults and youth: Thursday from 18:00

Summer Camp

Every year we organize a summer chess camp (e.g., in Rynoltice). The program includes a traditional chess pentathlon, which features tournaments in classical chess, rapid, blitz, and Fischer random chess, plus many other sports and fun activities.
