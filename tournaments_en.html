Tournaments and Competitions

Tournament calendar and information for competitive players.

Regular tournaments

We are proud organizers of many tournaments in the Liberec region. Below you will find our main events.

Open Desko Liberec

The traditional summer open usually takes place in July at the Industrial School. In addition to the nine-round main tournament (calculated for FIDE and national ELO), the event also includes side tournaments in Fischer random chess and blitz.

Rapid Liberec Open

A popular autumn rapid tournament that has already completed an impressive 28 editions. The tournament is open to the general public and is traditionally attended by strong players and promising juniors.

City League and online tournaments

We regularly organize the City League and, if necessary, we organize online rapid tournaments for members and friends of the club via the Lichess platform.
