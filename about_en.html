About our club

History, passion for the game, and the chess community in Liberec.

Who are we?

The Desko Liberec Chess Club is a traditional chess club with a long history in the Liberec region. Since 1994, we have brought together players of all age categories, from complete beginners to experienced masters.

Our main goal is the popularization of chess in our city and raising new talents within our youth base.

Our achievements and history

Every year we organize the popular Open Desko Liberec tournament, regularly attended by over 60 players, including international guests and grandmasters. Our rapid tournament Liberec Open has an impressive 28 editions behind it.

In league competitions, we have managed to maintain a high level for a long time, and our youth players regularly collect successes at regional championships and the Czech Republic team championships.

Playing room

Our home base is the Industrial School in Liberec (Strojní průmyslovka). Regular training sessions and most of our home tournaments take place here.

Come and see us at the next training or play in one of our rapid tournaments!
